## Brand & Style

This design system blends artisanal Moroccan heritage with high-precision digital fabrication (Bambu Lab 3D printing). The aesthetic is warm minimalism rooted in architectural restraint, organic sand tones, and sharp technological precision. It balances cultural warmth with high-end luxury e-commerce standards.

Key brand pillars:
- **Craftsmanship meets Tech:** Tactile materiality, clean geometries, and precise layer-finish cues that highlight fine industrial design.
- **Moroccan Warmth:** Ivory and alabaster foundations evoke tadelakt and zellige surfaces, accented by rich anthracite slate and the electrical punch of Majorelle blue.
- **Bilingual Poise:** Symmetrical, dignified layout handling French and Arabic typography seamlessly without visual friction.

## Colors

The palette avoids stark sterile whites in favor of warm, mineral canvas tones (`#FDFBF7` and `#F5EFEB`) inspired by natural sand, limestone, and unglazed ceramic.

- **Primary (`#1E2022`):** Dense architectural anthracite used for typography, strong frames, primary purchase CTA buttons, and stark functional contrast.
- **Secondary (`#1E40AF`):** Iconic Majorelle blue, used deliberately for focal interaction states, active filament selectors, customizable badges, and promotional highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D9A93C`):** Subdued brass/warm gold reflecting Moroccan metalwork, reserved for luxury certifications, edition numbers, and premium finishes.
- **Neutral (`#F5EFEB`):** Soft sand foundation used across card backgrounds, drawer canvases, and segmented pill groups.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Keep strictly to `#E6DFD5` for quiet separation that holds structure without harshness.

## Typography

The type system pairs modern transitional serif elegance (`Noto Serif`) with the geometric utility and crisp metric structure of `Manrope`.

- **Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Conveys editorial sophistication, art curation, and timeless luxury. Used for hero statements, collection headings, and product naming.
- **Body & Specs (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean readability for material technicalities (filament composition, nozzle precision, layer heights), dimension measurements, and checkout details.
- **Price Displays:** Always rendered in bold geometric sans (`Manrope`) with currency tags styled uniformly (`280 MAD` or `د.م.`).
- **Bilingual Pairings:** Latin and Arabic text should align visually on baseline; Arabic sub-labels and custom engraving previews inherit identical tone, remaining dignified and uncrowded.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured fluid 12-column grid system governs desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet and 2 columns on mobile. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with 24px gutters, max-width container capped at 1440px with generous section padding (`space-2xl` / 64px) to preserve gallery calmness.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6 columns with 16px gutters, collapsible sticky sidebar filters transitioning into slide-over drawers.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 2 columns with 12px gutters and 16px lateral padding. Product grids display as 2 columns, providing a compact browse experience without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes tactile layered surfaces and fine 1px warm outlines (`#E6DFD5`) over heavy synthetic shadows.

- **Base Layer:** The global page canvas rests on `#FDFBF7`.
- **Card Tier:** Surfaces elevate via micro-tint shift to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or soft ivory sand (`#F5EFEB`), encased in low-contrast crisp borders (`1px solid #E6DFD5`).
- **Floating Overlays & Cart Drawer:** Surfaces utilize high-performance backdrop blurs combined with soft ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive Depth:** Hovered product items do not dramatically raise; instead, they reveal smooth secondary image switches and a delicate border highlight in `#1E2022` or `#1E40AF`.

## Shapes

The design uses soft, subtle rounding (`roundedness: 1` — 4px base radius) to reflect precision machining and architectural stoneware:

- **Cards & Containers:** `4px` to `8px` corner radii, retaining crisp lines that echo geometric CAD models and architectural tiling.
- **Badges & Swatches:** Filament color circles remain 100% circular (`pill`), while technical tags (Bambu Lab print specs, customization badges) utilize sharp 4px corners with uppercase micro-type.
- **Action Buttons:** Subtle 4px radius on primary buttons; pills reserved exclusively for toggle chips and filter items.

## Components

### Product Cards (Aspect Ratio 4:5)
- **Visual Container:** 4:5 vertical proportion to highlight tall sculptural vases, bespoke desk organizers, and calligraphy lithophanes. Background set to `#F5EFEB`.
- **Image Treatment:** Crisp cutout product photography on seamless sand backgrounds with smooth hover reveal of second angle or lifestyle context.
- **Customization Badge:** Pinned top-left (`space-xs` from edge); pill-shaped badge in `#1E2022` text on `#FFFFFF` with micro-icon indicating "Prénom Latin / Arabe", "QR Code", or "Plaque Découpée".
- **Color Swatches:** Row of 16px circular filament chips (e.g., Noir Mat, Blanc Albâtre, Bleu Majorelle, Terre Cuite) with 2px ring spacing on the active selection.

### Customization Input & Arabic Preview
- **Bilingual Text Field:** Integrated toggle switch (FR / AR) that dynamically switches font baseline and right-to-left layout for engraving preview.
- **Interactive Live Box:** Sand background (`#F5EFEB`) displaying simulated 3D extruded lettering in real time.

### Buttons & Chips
- **Primary CTA:** Solid anthracite `#1E2022` background, white label, 4px border radius, 48px height. On hover: shifts seamlessly to `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Pure white background, 1px solid border `#1E2022`, anthracite label.
- **Filter Chips:** 32px height, soft pill contour, active state indicated by Majorelle `#1E40AF` fill with white text, inactive state in `#F5EFEB` with `#686D76` label.

### Slide-Over Cart Drawer (Panier Latéral)
- **Container:** Full-height right drawer (width: 420px max on desktop, 100% on mobile) over backdrop blur.
- **Free Shipping Meter:** Clean linear progress bar tracking progress toward free domestic shipping thresholds (e.g., "Plus que 70 MAD pour la livraison gratuite").
- **Item Rows:** Compact 64px 4:5 thumbnail, item title in `Noto Serif`, options pill (e.g., "Bleu Majorelle • Gravure: كريم"), inline numeric steppers, and bold MAD prices.
